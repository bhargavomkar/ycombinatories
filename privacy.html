Early Access Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy describes the information requested in the Veriidia Early Access form and how it is intended to be used. Veriidia is an early-stage product. The application service is not active until the database credentials are configured and verified.

Information you provide

If the form is submitted while the service is configured, Veriidia collects your name, email, country, individual or business interest, optional company name, your description of an administrative problem, optional context, and your consent record. Please do not submit government ID numbers, passport details, passwords, one-time codes, or other sensitive information.

Use and storage

Application information is used to review Early Access interest and, where appropriate, contact you about Veriidia. Configured applications are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted by the provider selected by Veriidia. The form does not currently send email notifications. If storage is not configured, the form reports that it could not submit and does not claim that information was saved.

Retention and choices

Applications are intended to be retained only as needed to assess Early Access interest and maintain an application record. This site does not currently publish a contact email or automated deletion-request process. Do not submit information if you need a way to contact Veriidia about a deletion request before one is provided.

Security and updates

Database credentials are intended to remain server-side. Veriidia will update this policy if collection, use, storage providers, or contact options change. This early-stage notice is not a representation of regulatory certification or legal compliance.
